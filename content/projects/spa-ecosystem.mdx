## Business Situation

A wellness business — often running several branches — selling appointment slots against therapist availability, and holding retail product stock at each location.

## Operational Challenge

Bookings, staff schedules, and stock were tracked across paper books and chat threads. No-shows and double-bookings cost revenue directly. Multi-branch operators had it worst: with no shared view of availability, staff, or inventory across locations, head office could not answer a question about any branch without calling it.

## Solution

SpaSystem covers bookings, staff scheduling, inventory, and payments in one ecosystem: a customer-facing booking portal, a cashier POS for front-desk operations, and a central service that keeps every branch's schedule, staff, and stock consistent.

## Business Impact

A slot cannot be sold twice, because availability is computed in one place from staff schedules and existing bookings — double-booking is prevented by design rather than caught after the fact. Any branch can be staffed and stocked from the same view, and an owner can see the whole group without contacting each location.

## Architecture

A micro-system architecture: a Laravel REST API is the core, with two purpose-built frontends — a Next.js cashier POS for the front desk and a Nuxt booking portal for customers. Availability is computed centrally from staff schedules and existing bookings, so both frontends always see the same truth. Each application deploys independently, letting the booking site iterate without touching POS operations.
