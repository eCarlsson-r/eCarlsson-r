## Problem

Spa owners were losing revenue to no-shows and double-bookings while staff tracked services, schedules, and product stock across paper books and chat threads. Multi-branch operators had it worst: no shared view of bookings, staff availability, or inventory across locations.

## Solution

SpaSystem covers bookings, staff scheduling, inventory, and payments for wellness businesses in one ecosystem: a customer-facing booking portal, a cashier POS for front-desk operations, and a central API that keeps every branch's schedule, staff, and stock consistent — eliminating double-bookings by design.

## Architecture

A micro-system architecture: a Laravel REST API is the core, with two purpose-built frontends — a Next.js cashier POS for the front desk and a Nuxt booking portal for customers. Availability is computed centrally from staff schedules and existing bookings, so both frontends always see the same truth. Each application deploys independently, letting the booking site iterate without touching POS operations.
