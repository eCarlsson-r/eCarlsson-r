## Problem

Insurance agencies were drowning in policy paperwork. Client records, policy documents, production numbers, and team bonus achievements were scattered across binders and spreadsheets — so answering a client's question about coverage or renewals, or an agent's question about their monthly production, took real digging.

## Solution

InsurancePortal centralizes policy, claim, and customer management for insurance agencies. Agents see their monthly production and team bonus progress on a bilingual AI dashboard powered by IBM Granite, new policies can be added by uploading a document that AI reads and extracts, and the whole portal works offline as an installable PWA for agents in the field.

## Features

- Policy management with AI-assisted data entry from uploaded documents
- Customer records with collapsible, structured data entry
- Agent production tracking per month
- Team achievement and bonus progress tracking
- Bilingual AI dashboard powered by IBM Granite
- Offline-capable progressive web app (PWA)

## Architecture

A Laravel backend with Inertia and React delivers a single-page experience with server-owned business rules. Document ingestion routes uploads through an AI extraction step (IBM Granite) that pre-fills policy data for review instead of manual retyping. The PWA layer caches the application shell and key data so agents can consult records without a connection, syncing when back online.
