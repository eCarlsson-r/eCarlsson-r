## Business Situation

An insurance agency managing client records, policy documents, agent production numbers, and team bonus achievements, with agents working largely in the field rather than at a desk.

## Operational Challenge

All of it was scattered across binders and spreadsheets. Answering a client's question about coverage or a renewal, or an agent's question about their own monthly production, took real digging. Adding a new policy meant retyping what was already written on the document. Agents visiting a client had no access to any of it.

## Solution

InsurancePortal centralizes policy, claim, and customer management. Agents see monthly production and team bonus progress on a bilingual dashboard, new policies are added by uploading the document for automatic extraction and review, and the whole portal works offline as an installable app for agents in the field.

## Business Impact

Coverage and renewal questions are answered on the first call rather than the next day. Policy entry becomes a review step instead of a retyping step, and an agent sitting in a client's office can consult the record without a connection.

## Architecture

A Laravel backend with Inertia and React delivers a single-page experience with server-owned business rules. Document ingestion routes uploads through an AI extraction step (IBM Granite) that pre-fills policy data for review instead of manual retyping. The PWA layer caches the application shell and key data so agents can consult records without a connection, syncing when back online.
