## Project Overview
A full payroll processing system handling employee records, salary calculations, and reporting workflows.

**Business Problem**  
Businesses need accurate payroll processing with role-based access and structured workflows to avoid errors and ensure compliance.

**Key Capabilities**
- Employee management system
- Salary calculation logic
- Role-based access control
- Payroll reporting workflows

---

## Architecture Overview
**System Structure**
- Backend: Laravel monolith handling business logic
- Admin Panel: Blade-based UI for operations

**Technology Stack**
Backend: Laravel, MySQL  
Frontend: Blade  
Infrastructure: Local deployment  

---

## Domain Complexity
Payroll involves structured financial calculations, validation rules, and role-based operations that must remain consistent and auditable.

---

## Key Engineering Challenges
**Challenge:** Managing accurate salary calculations  
**Solution:** Structured calculation logic with validation  
**Outcome:** Consistent and reliable payroll processing  

---

## Design Decisions
Chose Laravel monolith for rapid development and structured business logic handling.

---

## What Makes This Project Unique
Focus on business workflow accuracy rather than simple CRUD operations.

---

## Lessons Learned
- Importance of validation in financial systems  
- Structuring business logic clearly  

---

## Future Improvements
