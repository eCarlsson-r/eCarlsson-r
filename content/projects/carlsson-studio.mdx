## Business Situation

Business owners who know their operations need to change, arriving at a software studio to work out whether software is actually the answer.

## Operational Challenge

Owners describe operational problems; software gets specified in technical requirements, and nothing bridges the two. A contact form collects too little context, so the first several meetings are spent reconstructing the business before anything can be proposed. Meanwhile most projects that do start are rebuilt from scratch, despite sharing the same operational foundations as the last one.

## Solution

Carlsson Studio replaces the traditional portfolio with a software discovery platform. Through a guided questionnaire, businesses describe their operations, constraints, and objectives in their own language. The platform evaluates those answers against the metadata of every foundation in the ecosystem, recommends the closest match, and generates an implementation roadmap instead of a generic contact request.

## Business Impact

An owner leaves with a concrete recommendation and a scope in one sitting rather than after weeks of meetings. Because the recommendation is derived from structured foundation metadata rather than from whoever took the call, it stays consistent — and it improves on its own as the ecosystem grows.

## Architecture

The platform follows a modern frontend-backend architecture. A Next.js frontend provides an interactive discovery experience, while a Java Spring Boot backend manages project questionnaires, business rule evaluation, foundation matching, proposal generation, and future client management capabilities. The recommendation engine operates on structured metadata collected from every software foundation, allowing the platform to evolve as new projects are added.
