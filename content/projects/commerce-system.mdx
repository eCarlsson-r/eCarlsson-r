## Business Situation

An independent retailer selling through two channels at once — a cash register or spreadsheet at the counter, and a marketplace storefront online — with a single pool of physical stock serving both.

## Operational Challenge

The two channels never agreed. Inventory drifted apart daily: products sold out online while sitting on the shelf, and in-store sales never reached the online catalog. Keeping them aligned meant manual stock counts. Every marketplace order also gave away margin in commission, on sales the business had generated itself.

## Solution

CommerceSystem replaces that patchwork with one retail platform: a central service that owns products, stock, and transactions, a POS for in-store cashier operations, and a storefront the business owns outright — no marketplace, no commission. Every sale, whether at the counter or online, updates the same inventory in real time.

## Business Impact

What the system says is on the shelf is what is on the shelf, so double-selling across channels stops being possible rather than merely less frequent. The margin previously paid as marketplace commission stays with the business, and a new sales channel can be added without introducing a second stock count.

## Architecture

The system follows an API-first design. A Laravel REST API is the single source of truth for products, inventory, and transactions. Two independent frontends consume it: an Angular single-page application optimized for POS speed at the counter, and a Next.js storefront optimized for SEO and customer browsing. Because all writes flow through one backend, transactional consistency and inventory integrity are enforced in a single place, and additional channels can be added without touching the core.
