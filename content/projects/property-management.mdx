# Project Overview

UrusProperti is an AI-powered property management platform designed to help property owners and managers streamline tenant operations, rental payments, financial tracking, and portfolio analytics through a centralized modern dashboard.

The platform combines operational management with AI-driven insights to improve decision making, reduce manual administration, and increase visibility across properties and tenants.

# Business Problem

Property management workflows are often fragmented across spreadsheets, chat applications, and manual bookkeeping systems. This creates operational inefficiencies such as:

* Difficult tenant tracking
* Inconsistent payment monitoring
* Poor financial visibility
* Delayed operational reporting
* Limited data-driven decision making

UrusProperti addresses these issues through an integrated AI-enabled management platform.

The system manages interconnected operational and financial entities across multiple business domains:

* Tenants
* Properties

* Receipts
* Expenses

# Key Engineering Challenges

## Challenge: Complex Relational Data Management

Property operations require deeply related entities across tenants, units, receipts, and expenses.

### Solution

Designed a normalized relational schema with scalable entity relationships and structured financial aggregation workflows.

### Outcome

* Maintainable domain architecture
* Reliable reporting pipeline
* Scalable multi-property support

---

## Challenge: Actionable Operational Analytics

Raw property data alone does not provide meaningful operational insights.

### Solution

Implemented AI-assisted insight generation and analytics workflows for anomaly detection and operational recommendations.

### Outcome

* Faster operational decisions
* Improved financial visibility
* Reduced manual analysis effort
